Rebar Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out bar counts, stock bars and bar mass from the numbers you type. The spacing, the bar lengths and the label mass are yours to take from your own plan and the supplier's label. It has not seen your plan or your site and does not know the bar size, the cover, the laps, the loads or any rule that applies to your work, and it gives no verdict that a layout is adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

The count truth

Bars laid from the start of a run at a fixed spacing, one at 0 cm and one every spacing after that. Run is in m, spacing in whole cm.

The stock truth

Stock bars for that many bars of one cut length, cutting each stock bar into whole pieces, and the offcut that is left.

The mass truth

The mass of those bars from the label mass per metre, using the bar length above.

Rebar Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
