Boundary: arithmetic only Rebar Math works out bar counts, stock bars and bar mass from the numbers you type. The spacing, the bar lengths and the label mass are yours to take from your own plan and the supplier's label. It has not seen your plan or your site and does not know the bar size, the cover, the laps, the loads or any rule that applies to your work, and it gives no verdict that a layout is adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 4 m run at 20 cm spacing, the stock bars for 3.5 m pieces, and what the bars weigh.

The count truth

A 4 m run at 20 cm spacing takes 21 bars, the last at 400 cm, leaving nothing to the end of the run.

The stock truth

The 21 bars of 3.5 m cut 3 to a 12 m stock bar, so they take 7 stock bars, with 10.5 m of offcut in total.

The mass truth

The 21 bars of 3.5 m, at a label mass of 0.62 kg per m, weigh 45.57 kg.
